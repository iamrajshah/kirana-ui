What Kirana should do next

An updated, evidence-backed product direction after barcode billing, held bills, the Decision Inbox, Modern Bazaar UI, import/export and tenant recovery became real. The next phase should make every sale provable, every stock movement traceable and every closing routine simpler for the owner-cashier.

Evidence before opinion

Grounding Context

Now delivered

Barcode-to-catalog resolution, configurable store labels, cross-counter held bills, bounded owner decisions, responsive Modern Bazaar UI, reliable navigation, import/export and guarded tenant backup/restore.

Counter gap

Billing is fast and idempotent, but UPI selection is not durable proof of settlement and invoice detail has no complete print, download or share handoff.

Stock gap

Inventory is still one aggregate quantity per variant. Packed and expiry dates print on labels but are not stored as received batches, so FEFO, wastage and traceability are impossible.

Owner gap

Ledgers and reports are strong, but cash has no day/shift custody, supplier reordering remains manual, and operational exceptions disappear into bounded cards or short-lived toasts.

1

I1. Provable Sale Closure

Counter paymentConfidence 95%Complexity Medium–High
Description
Give each sale a visible lifecycle: payment initiated, awaiting confirmation, confirmed or manually verified, followed by print, download, share, reopen or start-next-sale actions. Keep payment truth and receipt rendering as separate contracts so a delayed UPI confirmation cannot falsely mark a bill paid and a printer failure cannot lose the sale.
Basis
direct + external: invoice and payment writes already use stable idempotency keys, but customer UPI presents a QR without a matching confirmation route and invoice detail has no receipt print/share/download action. Mature POS systems distinguish pending payment from completed customer handoff.
Rationale
This closes the most important remaining counter loop: the cashier can always answer whether money arrived, recover after a partial failure and hand over a receipt without reconstructing the transaction.
Downsides
Provider-confirmed UPI needs a gateway contract and reconciliation. A first release should support explicit manual verification with actor/reference evidence and receipt printing independently.
2

I2. Batch-Aware Stock Movement Journal

Stock lifecycleConfidence 94%Complexity High
Description
Record stock changes as movements and optionally associate received quantities with a batch carrying purchase cost, packed date, expiry and supplier reference. Keep the cashier view simple while inventory highlights sell-first stock, expired stock and batch history.
Basis
direct + external: current inventory is one aggregate quantity per variant, purchase receipt increments that quantity, and barcode packed/expiry dates are transient print settings. FEFO-style batch handling is an established pattern for expiring retail stock.
Rationale
One truthful stock history unlocks expiry warnings, wastage, returns, valuation, profit accuracy and reliable store-packed labels instead of building each feature on a different approximation.
Downsides
This is a meaningful schema and migration change. Batch capture must be optional for ordinary products and fast during purchasing or it will become unwanted warehouse ceremony.
3

I3. Solo-Owner Day Close

Cash accountabilityConfidence 92%Complexity High
Description
Treat the default session as the owner’s business day: optional opening cash, cash in/out, expected cash, counted closing cash, variance and a short explanation. Staff/counter handovers activate only when the shop uses them; the owner-cashier path can auto-start on the first cash transaction.
Basis
direct + external: Kirana’s cashbook groups by payment mode but has no cashier, counter, device or shift custody. Invoices already carry creator and counter provenance, and teller/register sessions provide a proven reconciliation model.
Rationale
The owner gets an answer to “the system says ₹8,420—why is the drawer ₹8,170?” without being forced to configure an enterprise staffing workflow.
Downsides
Payments and commercial events need durable actor/session attribution. Cash refunds, paid-in/out reasons and handover semantics must be settled before reports can be trusted.
4

I4. Explainable Supplier Cart

ReplenishmentConfidence 91%Complexity Medium–High
Description
Turn low-stock signals into reviewable supplier-grouped carts. Each line shows current stock, recent movement, suggested quantity, pack rounding, cost evidence and confidence; missing supplier or pack information stays in an explicit Owner decision lane instead of being guessed.
Basis
direct + external: Kirana already has thresholds, sales movement, purchases and suppliers, but no supplier-item profile, pack size, lead time or preferred supplier. Lightspeed, Zoho and Shopify show the value of calculated need plus supplier and item importance.
Rationale
The app progresses from saying “stock is low” to preparing tomorrow’s supplier calls while keeping scarce-cash decisions with the shopkeeper.
Downsides
Initial suggestions will have limited confidence. Supplier-item metadata should be learned during ordinary purchase work rather than introduced as a large setup exercise.
5

I5. Provider-Free WhatsApp List Compiler

Customer intakeConfidence 90%Complexity Medium
Description
Add Paste or Share List first: preserve the customer’s original text, propose catalog matches with confidence and alternatives, flag unclear units or substitutions, then create one held bill only after staff confirmation. Add screenshot OCR next; Meta transport remains an optional paid upgrade.
Basis
direct + external: held bills already provide durable claim/resume/takeover, while Meta APIs and on-device OCR make later text/image intake possible. Starting with paste/share proves the workflow without recurring provider charges.
Rationale
This removes retyping from a behavior customers already use while ensuring unstructured chat never bypasses catalog, stock, tenant or invoice controls.
Downsides
Matching must expose uncertainty rather than invent products. Multilingual quantities, local product nicknames and substitutions require careful evaluation before image/OCR intake expands.
6

I6. Persistent Owner Action Queue

Owner controlConfidence 84%Complexity Medium
Description
Evolve operational exceptions—not ordinary success/error feedback—into durable records with reason, priority, age, ownership, snooze, deep link and resolution evidence. Feed it from overdue dues, held bills, cash variance, failed sync, expiring stock, approval requests and stale backups.
Basis
direct + reasoned: the current Decision Inbox proves that record-linked actions work but covers only three bounded groups; notifications are transient in-memory events. Durable exceptions give new features one owner-facing workflow instead of more dashboards.
Rationale
The owner can leave the counter, return later and still know the few unresolved actions protecting the most money today.
Downsides
A queue becomes noise if every event creates work. Producers need strict thresholds, deduplication and clear automatic resolution; normal confirmation toasts remain transient.
7

I7. Safe Offline Sales Outbox

ContinuityConfidence 87%Complexity Very High
Description
Allow only paid-in-full walk-in sales from a versioned local catalog snapshot when connectivity fails. Store each bill in a durable outbox with Pending, Syncing, Synced or Needs attention states and replay it through the existing invoice/payment idempotency keys.
Basis
direct + external: Kirana already has stable invoice and payment idempotency but no business outbox, service-worker transaction queue or IndexedDB persistence. Mature POS systems expose offline mode, limits and pending work explicitly.
Rationale
A short outage stops being a shop-closing event without pretending that credit, returns, purchasing or stock administration can safely operate without server authority.
Downsides
This is the most expensive survivor: secure storage, device identity, catalog snapshots, conflict handling and reconnection tests are mandatory. It should remain last in the sequence.

Direction, not implementation plan

Suggested Sequence

Directional only — this shows sequencing logic, not committed scope or an implementation specification. Provable Sale Closure is the strongest immediate product conversation; the Safe Offline Sales Outbox remains last because it depends on disciplined payment, device and replay contracts.
